## A tiny hello from Aniraku

Aniraku is for the moment when you want to watch *something good* without getting lost in a noisy room. It brings discovery, a personal library, episode context, and source-aware playback into one calm little orbit—so you can follow a story, keep your place, and come back when you are ready.

> **Our pookie promise:** fewer sharp edges, more story time, and enough context to remember why you fell in love with an anime in the first place.

## A fresh little signal

### Native Android / v4.2

The current stable Android release is **v4.2**. It keeps discovery, relationships, eligible direct downloads, AniSkip, fullscreen, list sync, direct-to-proxy-to-verified-embed playback recovery, and the compact Audio & Provider controls close to the episode. This release also prevents a routine Watch-history refresh from seeking backward after a rebuffer, so the active forward buffer stays where it belongs.

## What we care about

| Keep | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Clear next actions, gentle recovery states, readable metadata, and real app behavior. | Noisy interfaces, pretend data, hidden surprises, and source claims we cannot honestly support. |
| Respect for users, contributors, upstream services, and applicable content rights. | Secrets in commits, unsafe shortcuts, and fixes that trade reliability for a pretty screenshot. |

## Make yourself at home

Good contributions begin with context. Bring a reproducible issue, a small improvement, a thoughtful idea, or a kind review; each one helps make Aniraku feel less like a product page and more like a place worth returning to.
